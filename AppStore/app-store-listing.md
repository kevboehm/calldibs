### Description

    Call Dibs splits a restaurant bill by what each person actually had, so nobody subsidises the table's cocktails.

    HOW IT WORKS
    1. Scan. Photograph the receipt, or pick a photo of one. Call Dibs reads the items, prices, tax and total.
    2. Call dibs. Swipe right on what was yours, or tick it off a checklist.
    3. Pass it on. Hand the phone around until the bill is covered.

    WHAT YOU GET
    • Your exact share, with your items, your share of the tax and your tip broken out
    • Tax taken from the receipt and shared in proportion to what you ordered
    • Service charges and fees shared the same way
    • Your own tip: pick the percentage, and tip before or after tax
    • Ordered three of something and only had one? Claim just yours
    • Shared a bottle or an appetizer? Split a single item between people
    • A running total of what is covered and what nobody has claimed yet
    • Fix anything the scan got wrong, or type the bill in by hand

    GETTING PAID BACK
    Add your Venmo handle and send each person a link that opens Venmo with their amount filled in. Or share the whole split as a picture in the group chat. Call Dibs never handles money itself.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    No account and no sign-up. The receipt is read on your phone and nothing is uploaded.
