# Call Dibs Support

Call Dibs splits a restaurant bill by what each person actually had.

## Get help

Email [kboehm89+calldibs@gmail.com](mailto:kboehm89+calldibs@gmail.com) with
questions, problems or ideas. If a receipt was read wrong, a photo of it
helps most.

## Common questions

**The scan got an item or price wrong.** Tap Edit on the bill screen and fix
it before anyone calls dibs. "See the receipt" shows the photo beside the
items.

**Someone forgot an item.** On the split screen, open their name and tap
Edit. Anything nobody claimed can be shared out with "Split the rest evenly".

**How do pay links work?** Add your Venmo, Cash App or PayPal handle on the
split screen. Each person gets a link that opens that app with their amount
filled in. Call Dibs never handles money itself. Pay links are offered for
bills in US dollars.

**Where are my past bills?** In History on the home screen. They are kept on
your phone only; see the [privacy policy](privacy).
